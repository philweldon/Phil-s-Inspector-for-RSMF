Privacy policy

Phil's Inspector for RSMF files · Effective 21 November 2026

In short: the app works entirely on your own PC. It does not collect, send or share any information about you or about the files you open. It contains no telemetry, tracking, analytics or ads.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Windows app Phil's Inspector for RSMF files ("the app"), published by Philip Weldon ("I", "me"), whether you installed it from the Microsoft Store or run the portable ZIP version.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not collect personal information, does not ask you to create an account, and does not make network connections.

Files you open with the app

The app reads the .rsmf files and folders that you choose. Those files can contain messages, names, email addresses and other personal information about the people in them. The app processes that content only on your PC, to check the files and show them to you. It never changes the files you open, and nothing from them is sent to me or to anyone else.

What the app saves on your PC

  • Reports. Each scan writes report files to a folder on your PC. By default the reports contain record identifiers (such as participant, conversation and event ids and attachment file names) but not message text or personal values. If you tick the option to include message bodies and personal values, the reports contain them.
  • PDFs. If you convert RSMF files to PDF, the PDFs contain the message text, participant names and pictures from those files.
  • Settings. If you tick "Remember these settings on this PC", the app saves your time zone, thresholds and report folder in a small file in your Windows profile. It never saves file content, and you can delete the file by unticking the box.
  • Temporary files. Very large files may be unpacked into a temporary folder on your PC while they are being checked or viewed.

These files stay on your PC. You decide where reports and PDFs go, who you share them with and when to delete them.

Microsoft Store

If you get the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download, installation and updates, and Windows may send Microsoft diagnostic information such as crash reports, depending on your Windows settings. Microsoft gives me summary statistics about the app, such as how many times it was installed and how often it crashed. I do not receive information that identifies you. Microsoft's handling of your information is described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Sharing

Because the app does not collect information, I have nothing to share, sell or disclose.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, I will update this page and its effective date before the changed version is released.
